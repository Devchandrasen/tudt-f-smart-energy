The summer test starts are 1, 5, 9, 13, 17, 21, 25 and 28 July. Winter starts use the same dates in November and December. Each block has 72 commands and 24 hours of prehistory. Measurement seeds are 11, 29 and 47. Conditions are Clean, Missing, Delayed, Noisy, Bias, Disturbance, Reference outage, Reference noisy and Common bias. Fourteen configurations provide 46,656 evaluated hours per controller and representation. The complete total is 1,959,552 records.

All study checks use voltage 0.95--1.05 p.u. and thermal utilisation at most one, with tolerance $10^{-7}$. The native SimBench bus bands include 0.90--1.10 and 0.965--1.055 p.u. Reported failures concern the imposed study band. Battery efficiencies are 0.94, state-of-charge limits are 0.15--0.90 and every block starts at 0.55. IEEE storage is 0.8 MW and 3.2 MWh at bus 17; SimBench storage is 2 MW and 8 MWh at bus 15. Buying prices are 70, 240 and 150 EUR/MWh during 00:00--06:59, 17:00--21:59 and other hours, respectively. Export receives 40 EUR/MWh and throughput costs 10 EUR/MWh.

\section{Paired block uncertainty}
Differences are left controller minus right controller. The stratified bootstrap draws eight summer and sixteen winter physical blocks, retaining all conditions and seeds within each selected block, for 10,000 replicates. The percentile intervals are conditional on the sampled benchmark periods. Multiple reported contrasts are descriptive.
